# Studio Surface Contract

| Owner | Route | Job |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `my-app/components/studio` | `/studio` | Create, inspect, iterate, and hand off assets. |

Required states: no capability, local or API capability ready, generation
pending, recoverable failure, result preview, and multi-result selection.

- Keep the composer primary and recent results visible.
- Keep status subordinate and assistant behavior supportive.
- Reuse the design-system shell and semantic tokens.
- Keep model management in Settings and video secondary to image creation.
