# Settings Surface Contract

| Owner | Route | Job |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `my-app/components/settings` | `/settings` | Install, connect, test, and repair creative capability. |

Required states: empty capability, ready or unreachable runtime, pending or
resumable download, saved secret, and passed or failed connection test.

- Group defaults, Local AI, API connections, language, workspace data, and
  diagnostics.
- Prefer compact status rows over marketing panels.
- Preserve the desktop keychain boundary for every secret action.
- Keep Settings subordinate to the Studio workflow.
